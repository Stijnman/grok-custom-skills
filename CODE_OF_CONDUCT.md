# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to a collaborative environment in which contributors, users, and maintainers are treated with respect. Participation in this project requires good-faith technical discussion, responsible disclosure, and consideration for the people affected by agent systems.

## Expected behavior

Contributors are expected to:

- Communicate constructively and focus criticism on ideas, code, and documentation.
- Respect privacy, consent, and intellectual-property boundaries.
- Disclose conflicts, limitations, and safety concerns relevant to a contribution.
- Welcome questions from people with different levels of technical experience.
- Follow the repository’s safety and contribution guidance.

## Unacceptable behavior

The following is not acceptable:

- Harassment, hate speech, threats, intimidation, or personal attacks.
- Sharing private information, credentials, or sensitive personal data without authorization.
- Submitting malware, deceptive automation, credential collection, platform-evasion guidance, or other unsafe content.
- Misrepresenting authorship, validation results, capabilities, or the provenance of a contribution.
- Disrupting discussions, reviews, or issue tracking in bad faith.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, remove, or reject contributions; moderate discussions; or restrict participation when necessary to protect the project and its community. Report conduct concerns privately to the repository maintainer or through the security contact described in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) when the concern is sensitive.

## Scope

This code of conduct applies to all project spaces, including issues, pull requests, discussions, and other channels used to represent the project.
