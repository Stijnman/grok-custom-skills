# RingTwice Power Suite

## Overview
This is the single most powerful operational skill for RingTwice. It is designed to give a service provider (particulier or PRO) systematic, unfair advantages in visibility, conversion, and earnings by mastering every lever the platform and clients care about.

It integrates:
- Deep algorithm & ranking understanding
- SuperTalent engineering
- Profile & keyword optimization
- Instant natural offerte generation
- Humanized communication (leverages text-humanizer principles)
- Review response system
- Job scoring & selection
- Local pricing intelligence (East Flanders / Laarne focused + expandable)
- Fiscal & status compliance (deeleconomie 10.7%, PRO vs particulier)
- Daily/weekly operator routines

## Core Ranking Model (as of 2026 knowledge)
The platform prioritizes roughly in this order of influence:

1. **Proximity** (strongest) – notifications prioritize closest available providers
2. **Response speed** – first serious responders win disproportionately
3. **Rating quality + volume** – SuperTalent threshold 4.8/5
4. **Realization / completion rate** ≥ 85%
5. **Recent activity & volume** (min 5 jobs / 12 months for SuperTalent)
6. **Recurring clients**
7. **Profile completeness + keyword match** to the request
8. **Price competitiveness**
9. **Status badges** (SuperTalent, PRO, verified)

Distance is hard-capped in practice (often ~30 km effective). Everything else is optimizable.

## Non-Negotiable Operator Rules
- Never cancel a job you accepted unless absolute force majeure.
- Respond to interesting requests within 5–15 minutes during active hours.
- Always ask for a review after successful completion (natural, not pushy).
- Keep realization rate above 90% if possible.
- Update availability and radius based on real capacity.

## Modules

### 1. SuperTalent Path
Track and engineer the 5 official criteria:
- Average rating ≥ 4.8
- Realization rate ≥ 85%
- Recurring clients (skill-dependent)
- ≥ 5 completed jobs in rolling 12 months
- Platform rules compliance

Provide monthly checklist + gap analysis.

### 2. Profile & Keyword Optimizer
- Rewrite "Over mij" and service descriptions for maximum match with real client search language.
- Suggest photo strategy (before/after, tools, smiling, local context).
- Generate category-specific keyword sets (tuin, schilderwerk, verhuis, montage, dieren, etc.).
- Output ready-to-paste optimized Dutch (and optional French) text.

### 3. Offerte Generator (Lightning)
Input: job description + postcode + your base rate or experience level.
Output: 2–3 natural, persuasive offerte variants that:
- Sound human and local
- Highlight relevant experience
- Give clear price (or range) + what is included
- Create urgency or trust without being salesy
- Are optimized for the ranking signals (speed + quality impression)

### 4. Client Message Humanizer
Takes any draft message and rewrites it into warm, trustworthy, neighbour-like Belgian Dutch (or French). Removes AI smell, adds natural rhythm, contractions, and mild personality while staying professional.

### 5. Review Mastery
- Templates for thanking 5-star reviews
- Templates for addressing 3–4 star reviews constructively
- Templates for handling unfair low reviews (professional + platform-aware)
- Strategy for systematically collecting more reviews

### 6. Job Opportunity Scorer
Score any new request 0–100 on:
- Distance / travel cost
- Estimated earnings vs time
- Competition level
- Match with your skills & ratings
- Client quality signals (if available)
- Likelihood of conversion

Recommend: Bid aggressively / Bid normally / Skip.

### 7. Local Price Intelligence
Maintain and update typical rate ranges for common jobs in East Flanders / Laarne / Gent / Antwerp region. Adjust for season, complexity, and urgency.

### 8. Compliance Helper
- Particulier vs PRO decision framework
- 10.7% collaborative economy tax implications
- When to switch to independent status
- Invoice vs non-invoice rules
- Insurance reminders

### 9. Daily / Weekly Operator Routine
**Daily**
- Check notifications first thing + every 1–2 hours during active window
- Respond or consciously decline within 15 min
- Update availability if needed

**Weekly**
- Review realization rate and rating
- Request missing reviews
- Adjust radius or rates if conversion drops
- Optimize one service description

**Monthly**
- SuperTalent criteria check
- Profile refresh
- Price benchmark update

## Workflow for New Job
1. Score the opportunity (Module 6)
2. If worth it → generate offerte (Module 3) within minutes
3. Send humanized follow-up messages (Module 4)
4. After completion → review request + log for SuperTalent tracking
