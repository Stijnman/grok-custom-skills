# Face recognition SOTA snapshot — 2026-09-10

Refresh these numbers with NIST FRTE pages before quoting them as gospel. Vendors move every few weeks.

## NIST FRTE (coverage through Aug–Sep 2026)

- Program split — FRTE (1-1 and 1-N accuracy) and FATE (presentation attack, morph, age).
- Mugshot identification — several leaders clustered near 0.1% displayed FNIR on high-quality frontal 1-N.
- Scale — Idemia-class result reported 1.11% FNIR at FPIR 0.1% on a 12 million identity gallery.
- Webcam-to-mugshot — leaders around 0.6% FNIR in 2026 writeups; still worse than mugshot-to-mugshot.
- Profile — still the hard split. Vendor spread is wide (low single-digit FNIR for the best, much worse for the pack).
- Residual errors called out by NIST staff — long-term aging and injury, not “the model cannot do faces.”
- Long arc — error vs 2010 cited as roughly 50–60x lower.

## Open models (not NIST closed-source leaders)

- ArcFace (2019) — still the default open embedding recipe.
- AdaFace — adaptive margin by image quality. Prefer on dirty video.
- TransFace / ViT face backbones — competitive on paper benches; LFW is maxed.
- InsightFace checkpoints on Glint360K remain the practical starting point.

## Do not quote

- A cosine threshold copied from a blog.
- LFW as proof of superiority in 2026.
- A generated lookalike as a biometric match.
