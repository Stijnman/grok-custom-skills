# Governance

## Maintainer model

The repository is maintained by its owner, who is responsible for reviewing changes, setting release priorities, and protecting the collection’s safety and quality standards. Contributors are encouraged to propose improvements through issues and pull requests.

## Decision principles

Maintainer decisions prioritize, in order: user safety and authorization, privacy, factual clarity, maintainability, and usefulness. A contribution may be declined when it duplicates an existing skill, has unclear provenance, adds unsafe automation, lacks validation, or creates unnecessary maintenance burden.

## Change process

Substantive changes should be discussed in an issue or pull request. Skill changes require a review of routing metadata, workflow quality, safety boundaries, and any bundled resources. Generated files such as `SKILLS_INDEX.md` must be refreshed before merge.

## Releases

The `main` branch represents the current published collection. External marketplace publication, broad distribution, or changes that could alter agent behavior materially require explicit maintainer approval after the checklist in [PUBLISHING.md](PUBLISHING.md) has been completed.

## Amendments

This governance document may be updated by the maintainer as the project and its contributor community evolve.
